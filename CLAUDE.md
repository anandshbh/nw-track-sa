# Reporting rule for the daily Investment Value Track update

Standing instruction from Shubhrant (do not ask again, do not explain):
after the update, the ONLY message/notification is one status line, in this style:

🟢 Green: 6 Nov added and pushed to main, no sheet errors, MAX and MIN unchanged
🟡 Yellow: <one line: e.g. sheet-error day added / needs manual upload / MAX or MIN changed>
🔴 Red: <one line: what failed>

No latest value, no change figures, no commit link, no verification details.
No new emails: say nothing or a single "🟢 Green: no new data".
